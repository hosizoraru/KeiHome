Final generation prompt:

> Create a sophisticated original app symbol for KeiHome, an Android tool for home-screen icon theming. NO letter K or letter-shaped monogram. CONCEPT: ICONS COMING HOME. A substantial deep teal home outline with a sloping roof and two short walls surrounds two prominent rounded-square app icons. One jade icon is seated at bottom-left inside the home, a second apricot icon floats slightly higher toward the open right side, tilted just 8 degrees as if being placed into the desktop. The roof/home frame must remain simple, one continuous thick rounded geometric shape, not a thin line; icons should be large enough to read as actual app tiles. Use negative space and the two differently positioned app tiles as the signature, a sense of arranging and customizing a home screen. Three large solid shapes total, flat fills only in deep ocean teal, pale jade and muted apricot. All parts remain recognizable when filled one solid monochrome. Plain warm ivory background; centered compact logo occupies the central 55% of square image. No extra ornament, no stars or moon, no typography, no branding text, no badge container, no 3D, no textures, no gradients or shading, no shadow, no photorealism. Make it feel like a polished desktop-customization tool rather than a real-estate or smart-home logo.

The vector reconstruction widens the small gaps and uses a 10-degree tile
rotation for optical balance. All illustration colors are flat fills; the
day/night palettes live in Android resources.
